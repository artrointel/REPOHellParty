# HellParty

Host-only difficulty mod for R.E.P.O. Adds independent difficulty levels 0–5 every three completed stages and Apex Predator clones with Crown identification and a Crown loot reward. Clones drop native radiation gas instead of orbs; lost clones are replaced one at a time on a shared 30-second cooldown.

Animal gains player-only native Cleanup Crew gas at difficulty 2: its current target must be within 3m, with a 10-second cooldown. Gas appears at that player's position when cast and stays there. From difficulty 3 (stage 10), two half-health Animals appear 0.5 seconds after its death instead of an orb. Each child drops one native orb if killed before its 20-second expiry, counted from its actual spawn, and cannot divide again.

From difficulty 1, Birthday Boy balloons automatically pop after a player stays within 3m for 3 seconds. Automatic, player and physical-impact pops spawn one Gnome when a living player is within 10m at the moment of popping. Difficulty 2 adds automatic-pop aggro and up to three Eyeballs from the newest remaining balloons on death. Difficulty 3 adds a burst of all balloons within 10m of the body on actual nonlethal damage, with one Gnome per balloon. Lethal hits retain the death reward rules.

Bella gains a seat-mounted Clown Doll at difficulty 1, linked to native bell warning stages. Difficulty 3 adds random Tiny bait on every bell pulse: the previous bait is silently replaced, every new grab of the current bait immediately starts Bella's native attack against that grabber. Bait disappears at 15m from a living Bella; killing her before it leaves that radius preserves it as collectible loot. Surviving dolls also remain collectible after her death.

From difficulty 2 (stage 7), Bowtie summons one Elsa at its wind target's position when the wind starts. The summon inherits that target, plays the native growth animation before attacking, drops no orb, and starts animated despawn 10 seconds after actual spawn (including transformation time). A new summon retires the previous one. Summoned Elsas ignore their own Bowtie's wind knockback/stun; their lifetime remains 10 seconds. The proposed three-hit retirement rule is excluded. From difficulty 4 (stage 13), Revenge summons an Elsa at Bowtie's death position after 0.5 seconds, prioritizing the identified killer, then the nearest living player. It shares the one-summon limit and no-orb/ten-second lifecycle; without a target it starts animated despawn immediately.

From difficulty 1, Chef checks for an unobstructed player within 20m once per second while roaming and disguises itself as a destructible native Frog excluded from drone attachment and Gnome valuable targets. Breaking it, including environmental destruction, kills Chef and awards its native orb. Body health and usable Frog durability share a percentage: the native 15–100% value range maps to 0–100% remaining durability. Damage persists through reveals and repeated disguises. Grabbing it releases all grabbers before restoring the same Chef with shared health reflecting Frog damage, then runs its complete native notice, aiming, approach, tell and jump attack against the grabber. While disguised, it checks visibility from the Frog every second and returns to roaming without an orb after no living player has been visible within 20m for five continuous seconds, including players hidden behind obstacles. Detecting any visible player resets that timer. After a grab reveal, Chef retains its target through post-attack recovery and cannot redisguise until that target dies or native pursuit ends. The 20m disguise range and custom ray checks do not override native pursuit expiry.

Only vanilla network objects and RPCs are used. Actual multiplayer compatibility still needs in-game playtesting. Release uses normal stage progression. The development Debug build forces difficulty 5 and spawns a Chef, test weapons and a valuable-targeting drone. Other planned monster abilities are not implemented yet.

The package icon is a temporary development asset reused from REPOPocketCartTurret and must be replaced before publication.
